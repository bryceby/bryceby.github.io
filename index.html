Boyang Wang

Tech Lead & Engineering Manager, World Model · XPENG

At XPENG, I founded and lead the Generative World Model (GWM) team. We develop X-World, a multi-camera world model for autonomous driving.

Before joining XPENG, I built and led Skywork AI’s Spatial Intelligence team, where I was the project lead for Matrix-Game and Matrix-Game 2.0.

I earned my master’s degree at Nanjing University in 2022. I received my bachelor’s degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Hebei University of Economics and Business in 2019.
